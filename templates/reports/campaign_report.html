Methodology & Audit Standards:
1. Net Sentiment Layer evaluates direct and contextual audience reactions, filtered through region-specific dialects (such as Pidgin, African slang, banter) and classified using local lexicon analysis matched with transformer-based deep learning polarity validation.
2. Audience Activeness measures viewer engagement intensity, representing views per follower ratio. It tracks active interaction behavior relative to creator scale.
3. Resonance Efficiency (RE) indicates organic endorsement success, computed by multiplying the Engagement Rate (CR) by the Audience Activeness score to benchmark true content performance beyond simple raw metrics.
